## Standard Bio (Long — under 750 chars)
KubeNest is an all-in-one sustainability data management platform built for Australian businesses navigating ESG reporting requirements. We help organisations centralise and manage their Scope 1, 2 and 3 emissions, waste, water and WHS data in one place. With over 10,000 built-in emission factors and support for GHG Protocol, GRI, GRESB and AASB S2 frameworks, KubeNest automates the heavy lifting of sustainability reporting. Our platform delivers audit-ready reports in minutes — not weeks. Whether you're a property developer tracking embodied carbon, a manufacturer managing Scope 3 supply chain emissions, or an SME preparing for mandatory climate reporting, KubeNest gives you the tools to measure, manage and report with confidence. Based in Hamilton, NSW. https://kubenest.com.au
