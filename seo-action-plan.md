## Hiring Red Flags (If You Hire Again)
1. "We'll build 100+ backlinks/month" = spam
2. "We'll submit to 500 directories" = worthless
3. Same password everywhere = lazy and insecure
4. Can't show Google Search Console data = not measuring results
5. Backlinks on irrelevant sites = no understanding of SEO
6. No content strategy = building on sand
7. Guarantees #1 rankings = lying
8. Reports vanity metrics (# of backlinks) = hiding poor results
9. Doesn't ask about your target audience = cookie-cutter approach
10. Pricing under $500/month = you get what you pay for
